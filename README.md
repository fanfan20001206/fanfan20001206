## 🛡️ About Me

I've spent **over a decade in cybersecurity operations**, living in the space between noisy alerts and real incidents. My day-to-day revolves around turning raw signals into something people can act on: threat intelligence, vulnerability discovery, alert triage, and clear, well-structured incident reporting.

I believe good security work has two halves: **finding the problem** and **explaining it so it actually gets fixed**. I care about both.

## 🔭 What I'm Working On

- 🕵️ **Threat intelligence** — collecting, enriching, and distilling OSINT and feeds into actionable reports
- 🔍 **Vulnerability scanning** — building repeatable pipelines around tools like Nuclei, from scan output to readable findings
- 🤖 **Office & workflow automation** — scripting away repetitive reporting, data wrangling, and document generation
- 🎬 **Video editing** — my newest side quest: learning storytelling, pacing, and the craft of cutting footage
